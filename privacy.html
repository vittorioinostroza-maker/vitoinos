Privacy Policy — Vitoinos: Animal Puzzles

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Developer: Vitoinos (Vittorio Inostroza) · Contact: vitoinos.games@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • Vitoinos: Animal Puzzles ("the app") is a jigsaw puzzle game that runs entirely on your phone. There is no account, no login, no server of ours and no cloud storage.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. Everything the game remembers (progress, settings, saved games and your own photos) stays on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process some device data to show ads (see section 5). That is the only data processing that involves a third party.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your phone, so the game can work. It is never sent to us:

  • Settings (sound, vibration, language, theme) and progress (completed puzzles, available hints, whether ads were removed).
  • Your saved game, so you can continue later.
  • Photos you choose with "My photo". The app opens Android's own photo picker, makes a reduced copy inside the app's private storage and uses it to build the puzzle. Your original photo is never modified. The app does not request access to your photo library, camera, microphone, contacts or location.

You can delete a photo you added at any time (press and hold it in the app), or remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Your own photos — your responsibility

Photos you choose are processed only on your device. We never see them, review them or receive them.

  • You are solely responsible for the photos you choose and for having the right to use them (for example, you own them or you have permission from the people or owners shown in them).
  • Do not use photos that are illegal, that infringe the rights of others or that violate someone's privacy.
  • The developer is not responsible for the content of the photos you choose, for how you use them or for any screenshots or copies you make and share outside the app.

4. Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details or your name. Google may tell us whether the purchase is valid.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows interstitial and rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information (model, operating system, app version), and your interactions with ads, according to Google's policies. The app declares the AD_ID permission for this purpose.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and where otherwise required, the app asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before showing ads that use personal data. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app's Settings → Privacy options.
  • Your choices: you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Non-personalized ads may still be shown.
  • Removing ads: buying "Remove ads" stops ads in the app.

6. Legal basis and your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

The app itself does not process personal data about you. For advertising, the legal basis is your consent, collected through Google's consent form, which you can withdraw at any time. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob; see Google's privacy policy to exercise rights such as access, deletion or objection. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

7. California and other U.S. states

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not collect personal information through the app. Ad data handled by Google is subject to Google's terms and to the opt-out controls in section 5. You can use your device settings to limit ad tracking.

8. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us at the email below and we will help you remove any data (the app stores data only on the device, so clearing the app's storage or uninstalling also removes it).

9. Security and retention

Because the app keeps its data on your device, it stays under your control and under your phone's protection. Uninstalling the app deletes all the data the app stored. We keep no copies.

10. AI-generated artwork

The illustrations in the app were created in advance by the developer with generative artificial intelligence (Stable Diffusion XL) and are included in the app. The app does not generate images while you play. Any resemblance of an illustration to real people or animals is coincidental.
